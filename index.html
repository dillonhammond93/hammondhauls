Common Questions

What you need to know
before you call.

How much material do I actually need?

As a rule of thumb: length × width of the area (in feet), times the depth you want (in inches, divided by 12), gives you cubic feet — divide by 27 for cubic yards. A 10×10 bed at 3 inches deep is about 1 cubic yard. Not sure? Send us photos and rough measurements and we'll calculate it for you.

How fast can you get to my project?

We work by scheduled appointment. Once your quote's confirmed, we'll find a date and arrival window that works for both of us — just ask about our current availability.

What happens to the material you remove?

Everything is hauled away and disposed of properly. Your space is swept and cleared before we leave.

I'm not sure what I need removed or replaced — can you still help?

Yes. Select "Not Sure — Just Ask" on the quote form, send a few photos, and we'll tell you what's involved and what it'll cost.

Do you serve areas outside your core zip codes?

Alpharetta, Roswell, Milton, and Johns Creek are our core coverage area. Just outside them? Call (478) 297-4448 and we'll let you know if we can make it work.

Can I get an itemized invoice for my contractor or realtor?

Yes — every job comes with a professional, itemized invoice, ready to forward to whoever needs it.
